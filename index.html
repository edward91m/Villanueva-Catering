Recipes that go back generations

Villanueva Catering started in Ventura in 2005, built on family recipes that have been cooked the same way for years. Every event starts with masa, a hot griddle, and tortillas pressed to order — not shipped in from somewhere else.

From backyard birthdays to weddings and office parties, we bring the full spread: bold, honest flavor and the kind of hospitality you'd get at a family table.
